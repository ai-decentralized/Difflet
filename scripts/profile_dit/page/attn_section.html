Routed attention per step, by phase

The routed self/joint attention (attn / attn1) of every block, times its executions per step, with the busy times of all engines added, so a bar can be longer than the attention's wall time. MM1 is QK^T and MM2 is PV; the quantize phases are the fork's MXFP8 preparation of Q and K, V and P (transposes, copies and quantize_mx). exp is its own phase here (in Plan A it was inside mask / softmax), and rows the two share count in both.
