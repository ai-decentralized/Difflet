Method, commands, paths and what was not measured
  • Time. An engine's time is the busy time of one physical core: the union of its instruction intervals (pipelined instructions overlap, so overlaps count once), semaphore waits excluded, then the mean over the two physical cores of the logical core (LNC=2: a logical core is two physical cores). Engines run in parallel, so their bars overlap and do not add up to the block. Collectives are the union of the profile's collective operations.
  • HBM time is the time of DMA instructions, whichever engine queued them (DMA queued on ScalarE or Sync is counted as HBM DMA, not as their compute). DMA values carry the profiler's own warning where it gave one. HunyuanVideo was captured without DGE notifications (its blocks overflow the profiler's notification queue), so its DMA attribution lacks the DGE metadata.
  • Blocks. A block NEFF is a compiled DiT block present on every rank and confirmed by a per-model signature (VAE, text encoder and embedder NEFFs rejected). It is replayed 8 times with its collectives on 4 workers; execution 4 is profiled per instruction and execution 8 gives the replay spread. Executions per step and the in-model median come from a system profile of a 1- or 2-step run (Qwen-Image and LTX-2 run 2 steps); LTX-2 and Wan run CFG as two sequential forwards, so 96 and 80 executions per step. Wan 2.2's high-noise block NEFF is Wan 2.1's, byte for byte, on every rank.
  • Components come from each instruction's torch module path; rows with none stay "unattributed" and are never folded into another component. Attention phases come from the attention kernel's source lines. The bottleneck of a model with two block kinds weighs each by its executions per step.
  • Commands. scripts/profile_dit/run_all.sh (13 lanes, one idle device each; capture.sh dumps, picks the block NEFFs, captures and exports them), scripts/profile_dit/analyze.py <blk> --model <key> (breakdown.json), scripts/profile_dit/collect.py --page /mnt/nvme/difflet-prof/page (results.json and this page's datasets).
  • Paths. Lanes: /mnt/nvme/difflet-prof/run/<key>/<arm>/ (dump, system_summary.json, blk<i>/ with the NEFF, NTFFs, summaries, parquet, source.json, breakdown.json). Committed: docs/verification/assets/2026-11-13-dit-block-profile/<key>/<arm>/blk<i>.json and results.json; write-up docs/verification/2026-11-13-dit-block-profile.md. Step times and quality: docs/verification/2026-11-07-mxfp8-video-models.md sections 7f, 10, 11, 12.
  • Not measured. The step times were not rerun here (host B for Wan and HunyuanVideo-1.5 is another trn3). No MXFP8 arm for HunyuanVideo-1.5 (no MXFP8 default). Only the high-noise expert of Wan 2.2. Quality at the profiled shape only where the evidence doc has it (no CPU fp32 reference at Wan 81 frames, HunyuanVideo 544x960x129 or HunyuanVideo-1.5 121 frames). The work outside the block NEFFs (embedders, final layer, host gaps) is the "outside the blocks" remainder, not broken down. Every NEFF carries the profiler's warning that it lacks compiler metrics.
