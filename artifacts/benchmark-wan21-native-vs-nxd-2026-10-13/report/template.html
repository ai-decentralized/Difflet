1 · Timing

Headline numbers

e2e is the difflet generate process wall, spawn to mp4. Cold means the OS page cache was dropped right before the launch with compiled artifacts present; warm is a new process with page cache and compile cache warm (n=3 at g=5, n=2 at g=1). DiT step is the inter-iteration time of the shared denoise loop, device-synchronised after every scheduler step, step 0 excluded, pooled over the warm launches. Lower is better everywhere.

4 · End to end

Where warm and cold e2e differ

Component times per backend for one warm and one cold g=5 launch. Bars show the difference: blue where TorchNeuron spends longer, orange where NxD AoT does.

Warm (127.2 s vs 107.2 s)

Cold, page cache dropped (737.6 s vs 432.5 s)

Cold start is disk-bound on both: EBS reads at about 124 MB/s. TorchNeuron reads the original fp32 safetensors (57.15 GB DiT + 22.72 GB umT5) on every cold start; NxD AoT reads its presharded bf16 copies (28.6 GB + 10.8 GB) written at compile time. Caching bf16 per-rank shards after the first load, or keeping caches on the instance's unused local NVMe, is the fix for TorchNeuron's cold start.

5 · Output quality

Are the videos reasonable?

NxD AoT's delivered videos are corrupted; TorchNeuron's are correct

Frame 0 of every NxD AoT video is fine; frames 1–8 dissolve into grid-patterned noise. The fault is in its on-device VAE stage, not its DiT: decoding the same NxD latents with the diffusers VAE on CPU gives a clean video. It is deterministic (identical bytes in every cold and warm launch) and not caused by VCS=2 (a launch with the variable unset produced a byte-identical mp4). TorchNeuron's video matches a CPU fp32 decode of its own latents to within h264 loss.

Measurements

fp32 oracle (diffusers on CPU, 2 steps, g=5)

The repo's W4 parity rule: final latents must have cosine ≥ 0.995 to the fp32 reference and an L1 error at most 1.15× what plain bf16 diffusers has (the bf16 control). Both backends start from bit-identical noise.

Blind review

Four independent vision reviewers saw frame rows labelled only X and Y (X was NxD AoT), each with a different brief. Scores 1–10, guidance 5.0.

7 · Method and evidence

Reproduce it

  • Both backends run the user-facing CLI with default placement (umT5 and VAE on device) and NEURON_RT_VIRTUAL_CORE_SIZE=2 exported to every launch, the NxD compile included. The NxD transformer trace reads it to pick the two-core attention kernel, and the stage cache key does not include it, so each backend had its own fresh cache.
  • The DiT step is measured by one probe on both backends (benchmark/wan_step_probe/sitecustomize.py): it patches the shared WanOrchestrator loop at import, so the NxD stage subprocess and the four torchrun ranks are timed by identical code. Every launch recorded VCS=2 in the process that ran the DiT.
  • Toolchains: TorchNeuron torch 2.14.0, torch-neuronx 2.14.3 (editable), neuronx-cc 2.28.4568, Python 3.14. NxD AoT torch 2.9.1, torch-neuronx 2.9.0.2.15, neuronx-cc 2.26.6360, NxD 0.19, Python 3.12. Runtime 2.35.27, tools 2.33.32.
